# Shopping category photos

The photos on the Shopping home's "Shop by category" tiles, one for each top-level category the API sends (`GET /shop/home`, by slug). All are from Unsplash, free under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license): commercial use is allowed, and neither permission nor attribution is required. They are credited here as a courtesy and so they can be swapped out.

- **Size:** each is centre-cropped to the tile's 84:100 shape, resized to 420 × 500 and saved as JPEG (quality about 74, under 55 KB).
- **Rules:** every photo shows what its category sells. No brand, logo or readable name appears in any of them. A category with no photo here shows its icon on the soft accent instead.
